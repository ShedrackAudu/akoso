# Legal Templates

Templates **for legal review, not final documents** (brief section 29). **None is
written yet.**

A Nigerian data-protection lawyer and a licensed Data Protection Compliance
Organisation must review every document here and the platform's NDPC registration tier.
Akoso does not claim legal compliance or certification. Each school is the data
controller; Akoso is the data processor.
