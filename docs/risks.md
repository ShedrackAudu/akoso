# Akoso: Unresolved Risks

Risks that the hard-questions review (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, section 14) could not
close with design alone. Each needs a founder decision, an outside confirmation, or
evidence from a spike or pilot. Reviewed at every checkpoint.

**Likelihood and impact:** High, Medium or Low, as judged before any spike. These are
judgements, not measurements.
